# Candidate emojis — One Emoji, One Smile

Judge brief (summary): a warmth judge shows two emojis at a time and picks the
warmer. Criteria in order: (1) warm and friendly, plainly aims to warm a
stranger; (2) warmth at first glance, no context needed; (3) universality, any
age/background reads it the same; (4) chosen rather than default — not the
first smiley that comes to mind, while staying instantly readable;
(5) right for a stranger — no intimacy, sarcasm or flirtation.

Also avoided: the most obvious default smileys (😊 🙂 😄) — the house judges
its 3 fixed anchor texts alongside every entry, and an entry byte-identical to
one of them shares its rate and never enters the playoff. A pick that is warm
but not the first smiley anyone would type is safer on that front too.

## Candidates

1. 😊 Smiling face with smiling eyes — the warmest classic smiley, but it is
   the default everyone reaches for, and very likely one of the house anchors.
2. 🙂 Slightly smiling face — gentle and universal, but too plain and too
   default; reads as polite rather than warm.
3. 😄 Grinning face with smiling eyes — joyful and open, but another first-
   resort smiley; loses on "chosen rather than default".
4. 😁 Beaming face with smiling eyes — a big happy grin; warm, but cartoonish
   and default-ish.
5. 🥰 Smiling face with hearts — radiates affection, but the hearts read as
   adoration/flirtation from a stranger; fails criterion 5.
6. 🤗 Hugging face — open arms offering a hug: warm, friendly, instantly
   readable, clearly chosen, and a hug is a greeting, not romance.
7. 🫶 Heart hands — "sending love": warm and modern, but newer (2021) so
   older phones may not render it, and it leans slightly toward affection.
8. 💛 Yellow heart — warm, platonic, universal; but a heart alone is a symbol
   of love, not a smile, and less likely to make a stranger grin.
9. 🌻 Sunflower — sunny, cheerful, universal, clearly chosen; but a flower
   is not a face — it charms rather than warms.
10. ☀️ Sun — bright and cheerful, universal; impersonal, no human warmth.
11. 😍 Smiling face with heart-eyes — adoring; reads as flirtation from a
    stranger, fails criterion 5 outright.
12. 🥹 Face holding back tears — tender and moving, can melt a stranger; but
    the tears add sadness and it needs a second glance to parse.
13. 😌 Relieved face — calm, gentle, peaceful; too low-energy to make a
    stranger smile.
14. 🫂 People hugging — two people in a hug, warm in spirit; newer emoji,
    busier at a glance, less instantly readable than a single face.
15. 🌷 Tulip — gentle and pretty; sweet, but not warm the way a face is.
16. 😇 Smiling face with halo — sweet and innocent; slightly odd from a
    stranger, with a faint religious tinge.
17. 🙌 Raising hands — joyful celebration; reads as "yay!" rather than warm.
18. 🐶 Dog face — cute and reliably smile-inducing; but random, not warm or
    friendly in the human sense.

## Pick

- First pick: 🫶 Heart hands — warm, modern, clearly chosen, and the most
  surprising of the warm candidates.
- Revised pick: 🤗 Hugging face — after a second look, 🫶 leans a touch
  toward affection ("sending love") and is a 2021 emoji that older devices
  may not render, while 🤗 is an open-arms hug: a greeting gesture, warm at
  first glance, universal since 2017, clearly chosen over the default
  smiley, and exactly right coming from a stranger. A hug offered to a
  stranger is the warmest thing one emoji can say without overstepping.
